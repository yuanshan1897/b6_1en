Share Your Discoveries

Take part in protecting Yuanshan’s archaeological heritage.

Explore the story

In 2017, the Yuanshan site was designated a national archaeological site following the Cultural Heritage Preservation Act to sustainably protect and manage the cultural heritage. We’d like to call upon everyone to help preserve this archaeological site.

If you find any artifacts when you visit the Yuanshan archaeological site, please leave them in situ. Removing artifacts from their original location will destroy the integrity of archaeological sites. A good way of keeping a souvenir of your trip is to take a photo of the artifacts and post it on our Facebook to share with the public and the preservation team.

You’re welcomed to share your photos and opinions of the Yuanshan archaeological site on our Facebook fan page.
